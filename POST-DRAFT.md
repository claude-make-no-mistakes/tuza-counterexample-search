Tuza’s inequality holds for every finite simple graph with triangle packing number at most seven. Any counterexample must have at least eight edge-disjoint triangles. The proof uses Puleo’s reductions and matching theory. [paper link]

Follow-up draft

If Tuza’s conjecture fails at packing number eight, there is a counterexample on 12–17 vertices. A maximum packing chosen to cover the most vertices must span a minimal counterexample, and the union of its triangles is connected. The same proof bounds packing nine at 19 vertices. [paper link]
